# Privacy Policy for Peak Translation

Last updated: September 16, 2026

## Introduction

Peak Translation is a Firefox browser extension designed with privacy as a core principle. This document explains how we handle your data when you use our extension.

## Data We Collect and Store

### Locally Stored Data
When you use Peak Translation, the following data is stored only in your browser's local storage:

1. **Saved Vocabulary**: Words you choose to save, along with:
   - The original word/phrase
   - Translation in your target language
   - Definition (when available)
   - Example sentences (when available)
   - Language pair (source → target)
   - Timestamp of when it was saved
   - Optional context snippet from where you found the word
   - Optional source URL and page title (only if technically feasible and privacy-safe)

2. **Extension Settings**:
   - Your native language preference
   - Your learning language preference
   - Audio playback preferences
   - OCR language preferences

### Data We Do Not Collect
Peak Translation does NOT collect or store:
- Your browsing history
- The URLs of pages you visit (except optionally when saving a word)
- The content of pages you visit (except for selected text you explicitly translate)
- OCR screenshots or images
- Any form of telemetry or usage analytics
- Personal identifiers or account information

## Data Processing

### Text Translation
When you select text and choose to translate it:
1. The selected text is temporarily sent to a translation service
2. The translation service processes the text and returns results
3. We do not store your selected text beyond what is necessary to show the translation
4. Depending on the translation provider used, your text may be processed according to their privacy policies

### OCR Functionality
When you use the OCR (Optical Character Recognition) feature:
1. You select a region of the screen
2. That region is captured as a temporary image
3. The image is sent to an OCR service for text extraction
4. The OCR service returns the extracted text
5. The temporary image is immediately discarded after processing
6. We do not store OCR images or screenshots

## Translation Services

Peak Translation may use external translation providers to deliver translations. When we do:
- Only the text you explicitly select for translation is sent
- We do not send surrounding page content unless it's part of your selection
- We choose providers with strong privacy commitments when possible
- We document which providers are currently in use in our documentation

## Data Retention and Deletion

### Vocabulary Data
Your saved vocabulary remains in your browser until you:
- Manually delete individual words
- Choose to clear all saved vocabulary
- Uninstall the extension (which clears all local data)

### Settings Data
Your settings persist until you change them or uninstall the extension.

## Children's Privacy

Peak Translation is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the extension's support channels or by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.

## Open Source Commitment

Peak Translation is open source software. You can review our source code at any time to verify how we handle your data.
